1 · Reference songs

Every target song needs a verified reference before real data collection: the tonic and mode of the original recording, its tuning (cents from A440), and the chorus melody written in the recording's key. Check each against the recording with a piano or tuner, then mark it verified and download the new songs.json. Commit that file. Its version is stored with every session.

SongKeyTuningMelodyStatus

Tip: play the original recording quietly and press Play melody at the same time. If the synth sounds “out of tune” against the record, adjust Tuning until it locks in.

2 · Test the scorer on your own voice

Pick a song, sing it, and see the pitch track and every score the study would compute. Use this to pilot the songs, and to check the scorer by deliberately singing a known number of semitones off.
