Study: Sing It Back: memory for the pitch of familiar songs. Researchers: [Faculty PI name], Department of Music, Stanford University; Akhil Reddy (student researcher). IRB protocol: [number — pending].

What you'll do. Sing or hum a few songs you know from memory, read a sentence aloud, slide your voice from low to high, try to name some piano notes, and answer questions about your languages and music background. It takes about 12 minutes.

What we save. By default we save pitch measurements computed in your browser (how high or low your voice was every 10 milliseconds), your answers, and basic browser information. We do not save the sound of your voice unless you tick the optional box below. We don't ask for your name or email. Data are stored on secure servers and will be shared with other researchers only in de-identified form.

Risks and benefits. There are no known risks beyond everyday life, though some people feel self-conscious singing. You'll see your own results at the end.

Your rights. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. To withdraw data you've already submitted, email the researchers with the session ID shown at the end.
