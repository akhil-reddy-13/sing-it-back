Study: Sing It Back: memory for the pitch of familiar songs. A Stanford Music 1A class research project by Akhil Reddy (student researcher).

What you'll do. Sing or hum a few songs you know from memory, try to name 12 piano notes, and answer a few questions about your languages and music background. It takes about 5 minutes.

What we save. We save pitch measurements computed in your browser (how high or low your voice was every 10 milliseconds), your answers, and basic browser information. We do not save the sound of your voice unless you tick the optional box. We don't ask for your name or email.

How it's used. This version is a pilot, used to test and improve the experiment for a class project. These responses won't be used in a published paper.

Risks and benefits. There are no known risks beyond everyday life, though some people feel self-conscious singing. You'll see your own results at the end.

Your rights. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. To have your data deleted, contact the researcher with the session ID shown at the end.
